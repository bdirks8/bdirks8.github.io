Biography

I am a Simons Instructor at Stony Brook University, mentored by Christian Schnell, and previously sponsored by the NSF MSPRF. For the 2024-2025 academic year, I was a Postdoctoral Member at the Institute for Advanced Study. In Spring 2023, I graduated with a PhD in Mathematics from University of Michigan. My advisor was Mircea Mustaţă. I am interested in Hodge theory, D-modules and singularities in algebraic geometry. This Fall 2026, I am on the job market.
